# India Cardiology Market Investment Strategy

## Project Overview

This project analyses state-level cardiology market opportunities across India to optimise Medical Representative (MR) allocation and maximise Return on Investment (ROI).

The dashboard enables strategic investment prioritisation using market size, revenue productivity, and ROI simulation.

---

## Business Objective

- Identify high-potential states for investment
- Classify states into strategic categories
- Simulate MR increase scenarios
- Evaluate break-even and ROI impact

---

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning & Preparation)
- Power BI (Data Modeling, DAX, Dashboard Design)
- What-If Parameters for Scenario Simulation
- Strategic Investment Matrix Framework

## Key Insights

- Maharashtra & Tamil Nadu show the highest investment potential
- Average ROI across selected states: 38%
- Break-even MR increase threshold identified
- Strategic segmentation: Defend, Improve, Invest, Maintain

---

## Strategic Recommendation

- Invest aggressively in high market + high productivity states
- Improve efficiency in high market but low productivity states
- Maintain presence in low-opportunity states
- Use the MR scenario simulation before scaling headcount
